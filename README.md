# MysteriousQoL

MysteriousQoL is a World of Warcraft add-on with quality-of-life features you switch on one at a time. It works on Retail and WoW: Forever. Almost everything is off by default.

## Getting Started

Open settings with `/mqol` or the minimap button. Settings are account-wide, so changes apply to all your characters.

- A welcome popup shows on first install, with a **Guided Setup** that walks through popular options (`/mqol setup` to reopen it).
- A **What's New** popup shows once after each update. The full **Changelog** is in the settings sidebar.
- The settings panel comes in teal, red or gold.

## Core Features

These work on both Retail and Forever.

**Quests**
- **Auto-Accept Quests** - accepts quests from NPCs, optionally skipping grey quests. Hold Alt to bypass.
- **Auto Turn-In Quests** - turns in finished quests when there's one reward or none. Hold Alt to bypass.
- **Auto-Select Gossip** - picks the gossip option when an NPC only has one. Hold Alt to bypass.
- **Skip Flight Master Chat** - opens the flight map right away. Hold Alt to skip.
- **Best Sell Price Reward** - marks the quest reward that sells for the most.
- **Quest XP on Experience Bar** - shows XP from finished quests on your XP bar.

**Loot, Bags and Vendor**
- **Faster Auto-Loot** - loots everything at once instead of waiting for the loot window.
- **Suppress Loot Warnings** - auto-confirms bind-on-pickup, loot roll and trade timer dialogs.
- **Easy Item Destroy** - fills in the DELETE text for you.
- **Auto Repair** - repairs at merchants, optionally with guild bank funds. Hold Shift to skip.
- **Auto Sell Greys** - sells grey items at merchants. Hold Shift to skip.

**UI**
- **Bag Upgrade Arrows** - green arrow on bag items with a higher item level than what you're wearing.
- **Max Camera Distance** - sets the camera to max zoom out.
- **Chat Copy Button** - a copy button on each chat frame.
- **Hide Elements** - hide alert popups, event toasts, zone text and red error messages. Hide Error Messages can keep important ones like bags full.
- **Mouse Ring** - a ring around your cursor, with size, class colour, combat-only and cast progress options.
- **Minimap Button** - left-click for settings, right-click for a menu, drag to move.

**Reminders**
- **Repair Reminder** - warns when your gear drops to a set durability, shown while resting or right after you resurrect.
- **Mail Reminder** - plays a sound on login if you have unread mail.

**Fun**

All sounds share one audio channel you can pick (Master, SFX, Music, Ambience, Dialog).
- **Batman Teleport Sound** - plays when you zone in by hearthstone, portal or toy.
- **Owen Wilson Loot Sounds** - a random "wow" when a loot window opens.
- **Vendor Grey Sound** - a money sound when greys are sold.
- **MystGems** - a match-3 gem game (`/mqol gems`). Also opens on flight paths with a bar showing time left on routes you've flown before.

## Retail

- **AH: Current Expansion Only** - turns on the Current Expansion filter at the Auction House.
- **Auto-Slot Keystone** - places your Mythic+ keystone when the font opens.
- **Skip Queue Confirm** - auto-clicks Sign Up in the group finder. Hold Ctrl to see the dialog.
- **Missing Enchant Highlights** - red tint on equipped items missing a permanent enchant.
- **Hide Social Button** and **Hide Talking Head**.
- **Skyriding Vigor Tracker** - vigor bar and speed display while skyriding. Hold Alt and drag to move.
- **Reminders** - Class Buff, Don't Release, Cheat Death, Dive, No Pet, Pet Idle, No Pet Sound, Overload and Imbued Mulch.
- **Ready Check Preflight** - on ready checks, lists what you're missing: durability, weapon buffs, food, flask, Healthstone and group Soulstone.
- **Sounds** - Blink, Bloodlust, Roll, Stealth, Encounter Victory and Zoomies.

## WoW: Forever

- **Upgrade Spell Ranks on Bars** - learning a new rank replaces lower ranks on your action bars.
- **Bag Space Warning** - warns when you loot with only a few bag slots left. Can ignore the reagent bag.
- **Bags Full Helper** - when loot doesn't fit, offers your cheapest item to destroy.
- **Thank Buffers** - sends /thank when a stranger buffs you.
- **Class Buff Reminders** - per-buff reminders for every class, like Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, Fortitude, Blessings, poisons and Aspects.
- **Seal Reminder** - shows your last used seal in combat when no seal is active.
- **Reagent Reminder** - warns when you're low on class reagents, with a setting per reagent.
- **Campfire Rest Reminder** - reminds you to rest at a nearby campfire while levelling.
- **Max Plainsrunning Sound** - plays music when Plainsrunning (Tauren) reaches 30 stacks.
